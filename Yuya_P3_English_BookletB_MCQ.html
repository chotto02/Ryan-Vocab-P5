P3 English — Booklet B Practice
10 sets · 20 questions each · Grammar Cloze · Editing · Comprehension OEQ · From 2025 school papers
What is Booklet B? Booklet B tests three skills: Grammar Cloze (choose the correct word to fill a blank in a passage), Editing (spot punctuation and grammar errors), and Comprehension OEQ (answer questions about a reading passage — True/False, sequence ordering, and short answers adapted as MCQ). All questions are based on 2025 EOY papers from ACS Junior, ACS Primary, Ai Tong, Henry Park, Maha Bodhi, Nan Hua, Nanyang, Raffles Girls, Rosyth, St Hilda, St Nicholas and Tao Nan.
